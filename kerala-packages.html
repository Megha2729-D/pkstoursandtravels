Kerala is a beautiful destination in South India, known for its scenic hill stations, peaceful backwaters, beaches, lush forests, and rich cultural heritage. Explore popular destinations such as Munnar, Alappuzha, Kochi, Kovalam, Wayanad, and Thiruvananthapuram with PKS Tours & Travels.

Explore Munnar, a scenic hill station famous for its beautiful tea plantations, mist-covered mountains, waterfalls, and peaceful natural surroundings.

Experience the beauty of Alappuzha, renowned for its peaceful backwaters, traditional houseboats, scenic canals, and relaxing waterfront landscapes.

Discover Kochi, a vibrant coastal city known for its historic landmarks, traditional architecture, Fort Kochi, cultural attractions, and beautiful waterfront areas.

Enjoy the coastal beauty of Kovalam, known for its sandy beaches, scenic coastline, relaxing atmosphere, and beautiful Arabian Sea views.

Explore Wayanad's lush forests, scenic mountains, waterfalls, wildlife, plantations, and peaceful natural landscapes, making it a popular destination for nature and adventure trips.

Visit Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala's capital city, known for its cultural attractions, historic temples, museums, traditional architecture, and nearby beaches.
